Full Crossing

3 Days 2 Nights Senaru – Sembalun

Mount Rinjani Trekking · Price on request

3 Days / 2 Nights
Senaru → Sembalun
Max 3,726 m
Challenging

Overview

The full Rinjani crossing starting from the rainforest side. You climb from Senaru to the Senaru crater rim, descend to Segara Anak Lake and the hot springs, climb out to the Sembalun crater rim, and finish with the summit at sunrise before walking down through the Sembalun savanna. Because the summit comes on the last day, your legs are already used to the mountain when you make the final push.

  • Rainforest on day one, open savanna on the way down — the whole mountain in three days
  • Afternoon at Segara Anak Lake and a soak in the natural hot springs
  • Sunrise from the summit of Mount Rinjani (3,726 m)
  • Camps on both the Senaru and Sembalun crater rims

Itinerary

Times are approximate and depend on the group's pace and the weather. Your guide will adjust the plan for safety.

This trek runs as a shared group: you join other travelers on the same date, together with our trusted local partner teams, and keep one AVENTRA contact on WhatsApp throughout. Want your own guide and camp? Ask for a private trek.
  • 06:30 Pick-up from your hotel in Senaru (other areas in Lombok can be arranged earlier).
  • 07:30 Registration at the Rinjani Trek Centre in Senaru, briefing, and meet the team.
  • 08:00 Trek through the rainforest to Pos 1 and Pos 2.
  • 11:30 Lunch at Pos 2.
  • 12:30 Continue to Pos 3 and up to the crater rim.
  • 15:30 Arrive at the Senaru crater rim camp (2,641 m). Sunset over Segara Anak Lake.
  • 18:30 Dinner and rest.
  • 05:30 Sunrise from the Senaru crater rim.
  • 06:30 Breakfast, then descend the rocky trail into the crater to Segara Anak Lake (± 3 hours).
  • 10:00 Soak in the natural hot springs near the lake.
  • 11:30 Lunch by the lake.
  • 12:30 Climb out of the crater to the Sembalun crater rim (± 4 hours).
  • 16:30 Arrive at the Sembalun crater rim camp (2,639 m). Sunset and an early dinner.
  • 19:00 Early rest before the summit attack.
  • 02:00 Wake up, hot drinks and light snacks.
  • 02:30 Summit push by headlamp on loose volcanic gravel — slow and steady.
  • 05:30 Sunrise at the summit of Mount Rinjani (3,726 m), with Segara Anak Lake below and Mount Agung on the horizon.
  • 06:30 Descend back to the crater rim camp.
  • 09:00 Breakfast and rest.
  • 10:00 Walk down through Pos 3, Pos 2 (lunch) and Pos 1 across the Sembalun savanna.
  • 15:30 Arrive in Sembalun (1,156 m), check out, and transfer to your hotel.

What to Bring

  • Trekking shoes with good grip (already worn in)
  • Warm jacket, fleece, gloves and beanie — the summit can be near 0 °C
  • Headlamp with spare batteries
  • Daypack (20–30 L) with rain cover
  • Trekking pole (recommended for the summit and descent)
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat
  • Personal medicine and a small first-aid kit
  • Passport copy (needed for the park permit)
  • Swimwear for the hot springs (3D2N & 4D3N)
  • Power bank and a little cash for tips or drinks
