3 Days 2 Nights Sembalun – Torean

Mount Rinjani Trekking · From $136 /person

3 Days / 2 Nights
Sembalun → Torean
Max 3,726 m
Challenging

Overview

A wilder way to cross Rinjani. You climb from Sembalun through the savanna to the crater rim, reach the summit at sunrise, and spend the afternoon at Segara Anak Lake and the hot springs. On the last day, instead of the usual exits, you descend the Torean trail — a dramatic route through a deep valley along the river, past waterfalls and steep cliffs, down to Torean village in North Lombok.

  • Sunrise from the summit of Mount Rinjani (3,726 m)
  • Afternoon at Segara Anak Lake and a soak in the natural hot springs
  • The Torean exit: deep valley, river, waterfalls and cliff walls
  • Quieter than the classic Senaru exit

Itinerary

Times are approximate and depend on the group's pace and the weather. Your guide will adjust the plan for safety.

This trek runs as a shared group: you join other travelers on the same date, together with our trusted local partner teams, and keep one AVENTRA contact on WhatsApp throughout. Sharing groups keep the price affordable while you still get full facilities. Trek Only: you meet us at the trek start. All-Inclusive: we pick you up in Lombok and you get a hotel night in Senaru — compare prices.
  • 05:00 Pick-up (All-Inclusive) or meet us in Sembalun (Trek Only).
  • 07:30 Registration at the Rinjani National Park office in Sembalun (1,156 m), briefing, and meet the team.
  • 08:00 Trek through farmland and savanna to Pos 1 and Pos 2.
  • 11:30 Lunch at Pos 2.
  • 12:30 Continue to Pos 3 and up the "Hill of Regret" (Bukit Penyesalan).
  • 16:30 Arrive at the Sembalun crater rim camp (2,639 m). Sunset over Segara Anak Lake.
  • 18:30 Dinner and early rest.
  • 02:00 Wake up, hot drinks and light snacks.
  • 02:30 Summit push by headlamp on loose volcanic gravel.
  • 05:30 Sunrise at the summit of Mount Rinjani (3,726 m).
  • 06:30 Descend to the crater rim camp, breakfast and rest.
  • 11:00 Descend into the crater to Segara Anak Lake (± 3 hours).
  • 14:00 Lunch by the lake, then soak in the natural hot springs.
  • 18:00 Dinner at the lakeside camp.
  • 06:00 Morning at the lake and breakfast.
  • 07:00 Start the Torean descent from the lake into the deep valley along the river.
  • 09:30 Pass waterfalls and steep cliff walls — the most spectacular part of the Torean trail. Watch your footing on the rocky sections.
  • 12:00 Lunch on the way down.
  • 15:00 Arrive in Torean village (North Lombok), check out, and transfer to your hotel (All-Inclusive) or onward.

What to Bring

  • Trekking shoes with good grip (already worn in)
  • Warm jacket, fleece, gloves and beanie — the summit can be near 0 °C
  • Headlamp with spare batteries
  • Daypack (20–30 L) with rain cover
  • Trekking pole (recommended for the summit and descent)
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat
  • Personal medicine and a small first-aid kit
  • Passport copy (needed for the park permit)
  • Swimwear for the hot springs (3D2N & 4D3N)
  • Power bank and a little cash for tips or drinks
