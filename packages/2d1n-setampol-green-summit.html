2 Days 1 Night Setampol Green Summit

Mount Rinjani Trekking · Price on request

2 Days / 1 Night
Bayan ⇄ Bayan
Max 2,608 m
Moderate

Overview

A different side of Rinjani, away from the main Senaru and Sembalun trails. Starting near Bayan village, the path climbs through coffee, vanilla and clove gardens into the forest and up to the green crater rim of Gunung Setampol. From camp you look across to Segara Anak Lake and the summit of Rinjani, with sunset and sunrise from the rim — a quieter trek for people who want nature without the crowds.

  • Camp on the green Setampol crater rim (2,608 m)
  • Views across Segara Anak Lake to the summit of Mount Rinjani
  • Trail through local coffee, vanilla and clove gardens
  • Sunset and sunrise, with Mount Agung in Bali on clear mornings

Itinerary

Times are approximate and depend on the group's pace and the weather. Your guide will adjust the plan for safety.

This trek runs as a shared group: you join other travelers on the same date, together with our trusted local partner teams, and keep one AVENTRA contact on WhatsApp throughout. Want your own guide and camp? Ask for a private trek.
  • 06:00 Pick-up from your hotel in Senaru and drive to the trailhead near Bayan village (about 30 minutes).
  • 07:00 Short briefing and start walking through coffee, vanilla and clove gardens.
  • 09:00 Reach Pos 1, Jebak Gawah (867 m), and enter the forest.
  • 11:30 Lunch around Pos 2 (1,445 m).
  • 12:30 Continue climbing to Pos 3 (2,134 m), where the views start to open.
  • 16:00 Arrive at the Setampol crater rim camp (2,608 m). Tents are ready.
  • 17:30 Short 10-minute walk to the sunset viewpoint over Segara Anak Lake and Mount Rinjani.
  • 19:00 Dinner at camp and rest.
  • 05:30 Sunrise from the rim — Mount Rinjani in front of you and Mount Agung in Bali on clear mornings.
  • 07:00 Breakfast at camp.
  • 08:00 Descend the same trail back through the forest and gardens.
  • 11:00 Lunch on the way down.
  • 13:00 Arrive back in Bayan and transfer to your hotel.

What to Bring

  • Trekking shoes with good grip (already worn in)
  • Warm jacket, fleece, gloves and beanie — the summit can be near 0 °C
  • Headlamp with spare batteries
  • Daypack (20–30 L) with rain cover
  • Trekking pole (recommended for the summit and descent)
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat
  • Personal medicine and a small first-aid kit
  • Passport copy (needed for the park permit)
  • Swimwear for the hot springs (3D2N & 4D3N)
  • Power bank and a little cash for tips or drinks
