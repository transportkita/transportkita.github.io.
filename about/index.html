About AVENTRA

Your trusted local Mount Rinjani trekking team based in Senaru, North Lombok.

Our Story

Born & Raised at the Foot of Mount Rinjani

AVENTRA is a licensed local trekking company headquartered in Dusun Ancak, Senaru, North Lombok. Founded by local guides and mountain enthusiasts who grew up exploring Mount Rinjani National Park, our team brings decades of combined trail experience and deep cultural connection to every expedition.

We started with a simple mission: to provide safe, comfortable, and memorable trekking adventures for international travelers while supporting local porters, guides, and mountain communities directly.

Why Trek With Us?

The core values that guide every AVENTRA trek.

Safety First

Certified local guides, high-quality double-layer tents, and complete first-aid preparation on every trail.

Eco-Trekking & Zero Waste

We practice "Leave No Trace" principles. Our team carries down all non-biodegradable waste from camp.

Local Empowerment

Directly supporting local porters and guides from Senaru with fair pay, insurance, and professional gear.

Our Senaru Basecamp

Before starting your trek, we welcome guests to our Senaru basecamp for briefing, gear checks, and pre-trek rest. We handle all island transport pick-ups (Lombok Airport, Senggigi, Kuta Mandalika, Bangsal Harbor) and provide free luggage storage while you are on the mountain.
