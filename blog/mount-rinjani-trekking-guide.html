The Ultimate Mount Rinjani Trekking Guide for Beginners

Climbing Mount Rinjani (3,726 meters) is one of the most rewarding adventures in Southeast Asia. Located on Lombok Island, Indonesia, this active volcano offers breathtaking views, crater lakes, and hot springs.

Choosing the Right Route

There are two main starting points for trekking Mount Rinjani:

    Sembalun Route: The best starting point if your main goal is reaching the 3,726m summit. It features open grasslands and a direct path to the crater rim. Senaru Route: Famous for its lush rainforest trail, leading to spectacular views of Segara Anak Lake.

Physical Fitness Preparation

While beginner-friendly options like 2 Days 1 Night are available, Rinjani requires good physical stamina. Cardiorespiratory exercises like running, swimming, and stair climbing at least 3-4 weeks prior to your trek are highly recommended.
