The Ultimate Mount Rinjani Trekking Guide for Beginners

Everything you need to know about fitness, routes, altitude, and preparation before taking on Indonesia's second-highest volcano.

By the AVENTRA team in Senaru · 21 October 2026

Climbing Mount Rinjani (3,726 meters) is widely considered one of the most breathtaking and rewarding mountain adventures in Southeast Asia. Located on the island of Lombok, Indonesia, this active volcano offers dramatic crater rim panoramas, turquoise lake views at Segara Anak, and soothing natural hot springs.

However, Rinjani is not a walk in the park. Even for active individuals, the steep slopes, loose volcanic scree, and cold high-altitude nights require proper physical preparation and mental readiness. Here is our complete beginner-friendly guide to getting ready for your trek.

1. Choosing the Right Route for Beginners

Mount Rinjani National Park features two primary gateways. Choosing the right starting point depending on your goals and fitness level is essential:

  • Sembalun Route (East Lombok): The default choice for those attempting the 3,726m summit. It starts in open, picturesque savannas at 1,100m elevation. While flatter in the first few hours, it gets very sun-exposed and steep near the crater rim.
  • Senaru Route (North Lombok): Begins at 600m in dense tropical rainforest. It provides ample shade during the hottest parts of the day and leads directly to Senaru Crater Rim with stunning views of the crater lake. It is ideal for non-summit packages like the 2D1N Senaru Crater Rim Trek.

2. Physical Fitness Preparation Routine

You do not need to be an expert mountaineer to hike Rinjani, but having good cardiovascular endurance and leg strength is vital. We recommend starting your training 4 to 6 weeks before your trip:

Cardio Endurance

Perform 30-45 minutes of jogging, swimming, or cycling 3 times a week to build lung capacity for high altitudes.

Stair Climbing & Incline

Train on stairmasters or steep hill trails while wearing a weighted backpack (4-6 kg) to condition your knees and leg muscles.

3. What to Expect on Summit Night

If your itinerary includes the summit attack (e.g., 2D1N Summit or 3D2N Summit & Lake), the climb starts at 2:30 AM in pitch darkness. You will climb from 2,639m (Crater Rim) to 3,726m (Summit) over 3.5 to 4 hours.

The final section—known as the Scree Ridge—consists of deep volcanic ash and loose pebbles. For every two steps forward, you may slide one step back. Staying patient, taking small steady steps, and using trekking poles will get you to the top safely for sunrise!

4. Managing Temperature Changes

Many first-timers are surprised by how cold Rinjani gets. Temperatures change drastically throughout the day:

  • Daytime on trail: Warm and humid (25°C to 30°C).
  • Crater Rim Campsite at night: Chilly and windy (8°C to 12°C).
  • Summit before sunrise: Freezing winds (0°C to 5°C).

Bring layered clothing (base layer, fleece, and a windproof jacket). Check our full Top 10 Rinjani Packing List to make sure you do not miss essential gear.
