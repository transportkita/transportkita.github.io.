From Gili Air to the summit of Rinjani

Gili Air is the island closest to Lombok, which makes it the easiest Gili to reach Mount Rinjani from. The crossing to Bangsal harbour is short, so you can relax on the beach in the morning and still be in Senaru, at the foot of the volcano, by the evening.

With AVENTRA you join a Rinjani sharing group — the best way for a Rinjani backpacker to climb on a budget. You share the trail, the campfire and the sunrise with other travellers, and you get full facilities: guide, porters, camping gear, all meals, permit and transport. Trekking Rinjani from Gili Air starts from $127 per person.

How you get from Gili Air to the trek

  • Day before your trek · afternoonTake the boat from Gili Air to Bangsal harbour on Lombok (about 15–20 minutes). We help you arrange the crossing.
  • At Bangsal harbourOur driver meets you and drives you to Senaru at the foot of Mount Rinjani (around 1.5–2 hours). Spend the night there so you start the trek well rested.
  • Trek dayMorning pick-up, registration at the national park office and a briefing with your guide — then you set off with your sharing group.
  • After the trekWe drive you back to Bangsal for the boat to Gili Air. Public boats stop in the late afternoon, so if you finish late we help you plan the next morning's crossing.

Your accommodation in Senaru the night before is not included in the trek price — ask us and we'll recommend a guesthouse near the trailhead.

Why a sharing group?

AVENTRA runs sharing groups only — no private packages. Sharing the trek with other backpackers keeps the price low, and many trekkers say meeting people from around the world was the best part of the trip. What you never share is quality: every trek includes full facilities.

  • Licensed English-speaking guide
  • Porters carry the camp and food
  • Tent, sleeping bag, mattress
  • Toilet tent for your group
  • Three meals a day, fruit, drinking water
  • National park entrance fee
  • Transport to and from the trailhead
  • One AVENTRA contact on WhatsApp

Your luggage

Only bring a small daypack on the trek — our porters carry everything else. You can leave your big bag at your accommodation on Gili Air, or bring it with you and store it in Senaru while you trek. Not sure what to pack? Read our Rinjani packing list and best time to climb Rinjani.

Questions about trekking Rinjani from Gili Air

How do I get from Gili Air to the Rinjani trailhead?

+

Take a boat from Gili Air to Bangsal harbour on Lombok (about 15–20 minutes). Our driver meets you at the harbour and drives you to Senaru — around 1.5–2 hours.

Is Gili Air a good base before and after the trek?

+

Yes. It is quiet and relaxed, and because the crossing is short it is easy to return the same afternoon you finish your trek — a perfect place to rest your legs.

Do you only run sharing group treks?

+

Yes. AVENTRA specialises in sharing groups. It is the most affordable way to trek Rinjani, and you still get full facilities on every trek.
