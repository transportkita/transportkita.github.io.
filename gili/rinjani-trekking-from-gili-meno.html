From Gili Meno to the summit of Rinjani

Gili Meno is the smallest and quietest of the three Gili islands — and a peaceful place to rest before or after climbing Mount Rinjani. Because Meno is smaller, boats to Lombok run less often than from Gili Trawangan or Gili Air, so it pays to plan your crossing a little ahead. We help you with that.

Our Rinjani sharing group treks let you climb Indonesia's second-highest volcano at a backpacker-friendly price. You trek with other travellers, and every trek comes with full facilities: licensed guide, porters, tents and sleeping bags, three meals a day, the national park permit and transport. Trekking Rinjani from Gili Meno starts from $127 per person.

How you get from Gili Meno to the trek

  • Day before your trek · afternoonTake the boat from Gili Meno to Bangsal harbour on Lombok (about 20–30 minutes). We help you arrange the crossing.
  • At Bangsal harbourOur driver meets you and drives you to Senaru at the foot of Mount Rinjani (around 1.5–2 hours). Spend the night there so you start the trek well rested.
  • Trek dayMorning pick-up, registration at the national park office and a briefing with your guide — then you set off with your sharing group.
  • After the trekWe drive you back to Bangsal for the boat to Gili Meno. Public boats stop in the late afternoon, so if you finish late we help you plan the next morning's crossing.

Your accommodation in Senaru the night before is not included in the trek price — ask us and we'll recommend a guesthouse near the trailhead.

Why a sharing group?

AVENTRA runs sharing groups only — no private packages. Sharing the trek with other backpackers keeps the price low, and many trekkers say meeting people from around the world was the best part of the trip. What you never share is quality: every trek includes full facilities.

  • Licensed English-speaking guide
  • Porters carry the camp and food
  • Tent, sleeping bag, mattress
  • Toilet tent for your group
  • Three meals a day, fruit, drinking water
  • National park entrance fee
  • Transport to and from the trailhead
  • One AVENTRA contact on WhatsApp

Your luggage

Only bring a small daypack on the trek — our porters carry everything else. You can leave your big bag at your accommodation on Gili Meno, or bring it with you and store it in Senaru while you trek. Not sure what to pack? Read our Rinjani packing list and best time to climb Rinjani.

Questions about trekking Rinjani from Gili Meno

Are there boats from Gili Meno to Lombok every day?

+

Yes, but fewer than from Gili Trawangan or Gili Air. Public boats run mainly in the morning, so we usually suggest crossing earlier in the day. Send us your dates and we'll help you plan the best option.

How long does it take to get from Gili Meno to Senaru?

+

The boat to Bangsal harbour takes about 20–30 minutes, then around 1.5–2 hours by car to Senaru.

Is the trek suitable for a Rinjani backpacker on a budget?

+

Yes. We only run sharing groups, so you split the cost with other trekkers. The 2D1N Summit trek starts from $127 per person with full facilities included.
