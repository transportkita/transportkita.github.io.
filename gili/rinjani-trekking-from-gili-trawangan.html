From Gili Trawangan to the summit of Rinjani

Gili Trawangan is where many backpackers spend a few days snorkelling and enjoying the island before heading to the big one on the horizon: Mount Rinjani. On a clear morning you can see the volcano from the beach — and from Gili T, the trailhead is only a boat ride and a short drive away.

Our Rinjani sharing group treks are made for travellers like you. You join other trekkers from around the world, which keeps the price affordable, and you still get full facilities: a licensed guide, porters, tents and sleeping bags, three meals a day, the national park permit and transport. Trekking Rinjani from Gili Trawangan starts from just $127 per person.

How you get from Gili Trawangan to the trek

  • Day before your trek · afternoonTake the boat from Gili Trawangan to Bangsal harbour on Lombok (about 30–40 minutes). We help you arrange the crossing.
  • At Bangsal harbourOur driver meets you and drives you to Senaru at the foot of Mount Rinjani (around 1.5–2 hours). Spend the night there so you start the trek well rested.
  • Trek dayMorning pick-up, registration at the national park office and a briefing with your guide — then you set off with your sharing group.
  • After the trekWe drive you back to Bangsal for the boat to Gili Trawangan. Public boats stop in the late afternoon, so if you finish late we help you plan the next morning's crossing.

Your accommodation in Senaru the night before is not included in the trek price — ask us and we'll recommend a guesthouse near the trailhead.

Why a sharing group?

AVENTRA runs sharing groups only — no private packages. Sharing the trek with other backpackers keeps the price low, and many trekkers say meeting people from around the world was the best part of the trip. What you never share is quality: every trek includes full facilities.

  • Licensed English-speaking guide
  • Porters carry the camp and food
  • Tent, sleeping bag, mattress
  • Toilet tent for your group
  • Three meals a day, fruit, drinking water
  • National park entrance fee
  • Transport to and from the trailhead
  • One AVENTRA contact on WhatsApp

Your luggage

Only bring a small daypack on the trek — our porters carry everything else. You can leave your big bag at your accommodation on Gili Trawangan, or bring it with you and store it in Senaru while you trek. Not sure what to pack? Read our Rinjani packing list and best time to climb Rinjani.

Questions about trekking Rinjani from Gili Trawangan

How far is Mount Rinjani from Gili Trawangan?

+

Gili T is close to Lombok's north-west coast. The boat to Bangsal harbour takes about 30–40 minutes, then it is around 1.5–2 hours by car to Senaru, or a little longer to Sembalun.

Can I go straight from a party night on Gili T to the trek?

+

We don't recommend it. The summit packages are long and steep, so give yourself a good night's sleep in Senaru before you start. That's why we suggest crossing the afternoon before your trek.

Is there a cheaper way than a private trek?

+

Yes — that's exactly what we do. AVENTRA runs sharing groups only. You trek with other backpackers on the same date, so the cost of the guide, porters and transport is shared, and the price stays affordable.
