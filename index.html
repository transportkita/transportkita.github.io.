What is the Best Time to Climb Mount Rinjani?

+

The best trekking season is from April to October during the dry season. The weather is clear, trails are dry, and views are spectacular. The peak season is July–August. Trekking is generally closed during the rainy season (November–March) due to safety risks.

Do I Need a Permit to Trek Mount Rinjani?

+

Yes. Entry permit to Mount Rinjani National Park is included in our package price — we arrange everything for you. You just need to provide your full name, nationality, and passport details when booking.

Is Accommodation Included the Night Before the Trek?

+

Hotel accommodation before and after the trek is not included in the package price. However, we can recommend and book hotels in Senaru or Sembalun for you at special rates. We also provide free pick-up from your hotel to the starting point.

What is Your Average Group Size?

+

We operate both private tours (just you and your group) and shared groups (max 6–8 trekkers per guide). Larger groups can be arranged upon request. Smaller groups ensure better service and safety.

Sembalun vs Senaru Route — Which One to Choose?

+

Sembalun Route: Starts higher up — steeper but shorter ascent to the summit. Better for sunrise. Recommended for fit trekkers.

Senaru Route: Gentler and more gradual ascent. Better views of the forest and waterfalls. Easier and more beginner-friendly. Most popular choice.

Is Trekking Mount Rinjani Suitable for Beginners?

+

The 2D1N Senaru route is suitable for beginners with reasonable fitness. The summit push is challenging but achievable with our experienced guides. The 3D2N and 4D3N packages give you more time and are also suitable for first-timers. We recommend training and walking regularly before your trek!

What Kind of Food is Provided During the Trek?

+

We provide three meals a day plus snacks and hot drinks. Meals include rice, noodles, vegetables, eggs, chicken or fish, soup, biscuits, chocolate, tea, coffee, and mineral water. Fresh fruits are also included. We can accommodate vegetarian and dietary requirements — just inform us before booking!

Are There Toilet Facilities on the Mountain?

+

Public toilets are available at some campsites but are basic. For your comfort, we provide a private toilet tent with portable toilet for our groups — you'll have your own private toilet throughout the trek. This is one of the ways we ensure a comfortable experience!
