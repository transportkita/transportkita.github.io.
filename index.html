Why Choose AVENTRA?

+

We are a local trekking team based in Senaru, at the foot of Mount Rinjani, guiding trekkers since 2018. We work with licensed English-speaking guides with years of experience on the mountain. We provide transparent pricing with no hidden fees, quality camping equipment, nutritious meals including fresh fruits, and reliable transport. Your safety and comfort are our top priorities.

What is the Best Time to Climb Mount Rinjani?

+

The best trekking season is from April to October during the dry season. The weather is clear, trails are dry, and views are spectacular. The peak season is July–August. The national park usually closes the trails during the peak rainy season, around January to March. See our month-by-month guide.

Do I Need a Permit to Trek Mount Rinjani?

+

Yes. Entry permit to Mount Rinjani National Park is included in our package price — we arrange everything for you. You just need to provide your full name, nationality, and passport details when booking.

Is Accommodation Included the Night Before the Trek?

+

Hotel accommodation before and after the trek is not included in the package price. However, we can recommend and book hotels in Senaru or Sembalun for you at special rates. We also provide free pick-up from your hotel to the starting point.

How Do Sharing Groups Work?

+

Most of our treks run as shared groups: you join other travelers on the same route and date, usually 6–8 trekkers per guide. To fill groups on every date we trek together with trusted local partner teams, and you keep one AVENTRA contact on WhatsApp the whole time. We specialise in sharing groups only: it keeps the price affordable, and you still get full facilities on every trek.

Sembalun vs Senaru Route — Which One to Choose?

+

Sembalun Route: Starts higher up — steeper but shorter ascent to the summit. Better for sunrise. Recommended for fit trekkers.

Senaru Route: Gentler and more gradual ascent. Better views of the forest and waterfalls. Easier and more beginner-friendly. Most popular choice.

Is Trekking Mount Rinjani Suitable for Beginners?

+

The 2D1N Summit package (via Sembalun) is suitable for beginners with reasonable fitness. The summit push is challenging but achievable with our experienced guides. The 3D2N and 4D3N packages give you more time and are also suitable for first-timers. We recommend training and walking regularly before your trek!

What Kind of Food is Provided During the Trek?

+

We provide three meals a day plus snacks and hot drinks. Meals include rice, noodles, vegetables, eggs, chicken or fish, soup, biscuits, chocolate, tea, coffee, and mineral water. Fresh fruits are also included. We can accommodate vegetarian and dietary requirements — just inform us before booking!

Are There Toilet Facilities on the Mountain?

+

Public toilets are available at some campsites but are basic. For your comfort, every group has its own toilet tent with a portable toilet, used only by your group throughout the trek.
