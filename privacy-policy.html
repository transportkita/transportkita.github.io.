Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Information we collect

  • When you contact or book with us: your name, nationality, passport number (for the park permit), phone or WhatsApp number, email, hotel for pick-up, trek dates, and any health or dietary information you choose to share.
  • When you visit this website: basic technical information such as pages visited, device type, approximate location and how you arrived at the site, collected through cookies by Google (Google Ads and Google tag).

How we use it

  • To arrange your trek, park permit, transport and meals
  • To reply to your questions and send booking confirmations
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising (for example, to know which ads lead to enquiries)

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share what is needed with the Mount Rinjani National Park office (for permits), our own guides and drivers, and service providers such as Google that help us run this website and our advertising.

Cookies and advertising

This website uses the Google tag to measure visits and advertising results, and Google may use cookies to show you relevant ads. You can manage personalised ads at Google Ad Settings and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep booking information only as long as needed to deliver your trek and meet legal or accounting requirements, and then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us on WhatsApp or at info.transportkita@gmail.com.
