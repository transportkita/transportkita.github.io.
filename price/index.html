Rinjani Trekking Prices

All prices are in US dollars, per person, for a sharing group — you trek together with other travellers. Choose Trek Only or All-Inclusive.

Two ways to book

Both options are the same sharing group trek with the same guide, porters, camping gear and meals. The only difference is how you get to the trek and where you sleep the night before.

Option 1

Trek Only

From $127 /person

  • The full trek with guide, porters, gear, meals and park permit
  • No transport — you meet us at the trek start in Senaru or Sembalun
  • No hotel before the trek

Best if you are already staying in Senaru or Sembalun

Option 2

All-Inclusive

From $135 /person

  • The full trek with guide, porters, gear, meals and park permit
  • Pick-up and drop-off in Lombok
  • 1 night hotel in Senaru before the trek

Best if you come from the Gili islands, Senggigi, Mataram, Kuta or the airport

How to read the price tables

  • Every price is per person. Your total is the price × the number of people in your booking.
  • The columns show how many people book together — for example you and your friends. The more people in one booking, the lower the price per person.
  • You always trek in a sharing group. Even if you book alone, you join other travellers on the same date. That is how we keep the price affordable.
  • No hidden fees. The price we confirm in writing on WhatsApp is the price you pay.

Example

Two friends book the 3 Days 2 Nights Summit & Lake, Trek Only: 2 × $155 = $310 in total. They trek with other travellers in a sharing group, and they make their own way to Sembalun.
